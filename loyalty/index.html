How It Works

Every purchase earns you points toward your next tier. Accumulate enough and your account advances automatically — unlocking better discounts, exclusive deals, and priority perks. Tiers are evaluated on a rolling basis so your status always reflects your recent activity.

Loyalty Rewards Program

How to Enroll

  1. 01

    In Person at the Register

    Ask any budtender or cashier to sign you up when you check in. We'll create your loyalty account on the spot — all you need is a valid 21+ ID. Your points start accumulating immediately on your first qualifying purchase.

  2. 02

    Via Your Dutchie Account

    Already have a Dutchie account? Your loyalty membership will be linked automatically once the program launches. No Dutchie account yet? Create one free at dutchie.com — it also makes online ordering and express pickup faster.
