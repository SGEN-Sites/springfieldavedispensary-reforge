Our Top Sativa Picks for Summer

Summer is sativa season. When the days are long and you want to be outside on a hike, at the beach, or just hanging on the porch, the last thing you want is a strain that glues you to the couch. We pulled three of our staff favorites from the current menu — all bright, energetic, and built for sunshine.

Durban Poison is the classic. Pure landrace sativa from South Africa, sweet anise smell, and a clear-headed buzz that's almost coffee-like. Great for daytime productivity and surprisingly social. If you have errands to run and want to actually enjoy them, this is the move.

Super Lemon Haze is louder and brighter. Heavy on limonene, with a citrus zest you can smell from across the room. It's an uplifting, almost giggly high — pairs beautifully with music, food, and friends. Watch the dose; it can be sneaky-strong.

Jack Herer rounds it out. Named after the cannabis legend, this is a piney, peppery sativa with a slow-build creative high. A lot of writers, artists, and musicians keep this one in rotation for a reason. If you want to be focused but loose, Jack delivers.

All three rotate in and out of stock — check the live menu for current availability and weekly 420 deals.
