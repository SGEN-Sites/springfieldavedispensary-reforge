Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a medical card to purchase?

No! We are a recreational dispensary. Any adult 21 years of age or older with a valid, non-expired government-issued ID (state ID, driver's license, or passport) can purchase from us.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash and debit cards in-store. We also accept Dutchie Pay (an ACH payment system) for online orders and delivery. We have an ATM on site for your convenience.

How much cannabis can I buy at once?

Under New Jersey law, recreational customers can purchase up to 1 ounce of usable cannabis (or the equivalent in other products) per transaction. This equals 1 ounce of flower, 1,000mg of edibles, or 1,000mg (1 gram) of concentrates.

Do you deliver?

Yes! We offer free delivery to the Irvington area and surrounding Essex County on orders over $60. Orders under $60 are subject to an $11 delivery fee. Deliveries must be paid via Dutchie Pay (ACH) prior to arrival. The person who placed the order must be present with valid ID to receive the delivery.
